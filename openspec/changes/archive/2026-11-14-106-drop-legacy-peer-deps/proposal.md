## Why

Every install runs with `--legacy-peer-deps` (README, `docs/*`, `lefthook.yml`, CI, the overlay `commands.install`). The reason is one package: the project is on `eslint` 10.6.0, and `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y` 6.10.2 (the latest) declares its `eslint` peer only up to `^9` (upstream jsx-eslint/eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y#1075, open). The flag switches off peer-dependency validation for the whole tree, so it can hide unrelated conflicts in any future install. The operator asked to remove it (OI-35, issue #106 opened on OI-37) while keeping the JSX accessibility checks; the resolution is left to this change.

## What Changes

- A resolution that lets a plain `npm ci` and `npm install` succeed with the accessibility lint intact (chosen in `design.md`: the ESLint 10 compatible fork `eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y-x` if its rules match today's set; otherwise an npm `overrides` entry scoped to the plugin's `eslint` peer).
- `package.json`, `package-lock.json` (regenerated by a normal install) and `eslint.config.js` follow the resolution; with the fork, `docs/eslint/README.md` names the new plugin.
- `--legacy-peer-deps` disappears from `README.md`, `docs/installation/README.md`, `docs/hooks/README.md`, `lefthook.yml` and the `minimatch` entry's commands; `docs/development/README.md` keeps its general guidance, reworded; the known issue in `docs/issues/README.md` is closed (removed, with the general "investigate a peer conflict first" guidance kept), and the `minimatch` advisory entry follows the new tree.
- Team rules, after the above is on main: the overlay `commands.install` becomes `npm ci`, and `ai-team ci render` updates `.github/workflows/pr-checks.yml` and `full-tests.yml`.
- If neither resolution holds: no package change; `docs/issues/README.md` records what was tried, why it failed and what to recheck (and when); the flag stays.

No product behaviour changes (`skip_specs: true`).
